Ability Animation Reference: fire_breath

Inferno Drake signature ability — a cone telegraph with a channeled damage zone. This covers mechanics the previous three didn't: arc/pie-slice shapes, channeled abilities that deal damage over time, direction-locked zones, and persistent hazard rendering.

0 What is fire_breath?

Used by: Inferno Drake (primary)

Shape: Cone — 60° arc, 5 tile radius, aimed at the target's position when telegraph starts

Damage: 8 per tick, 4 ticks over the channel (32 total) + burn DoT (3 damage/sec for 3 seconds = 9 more)

Telegraph: 600ms — orange cone outline appears on the ground, direction locks immediately

Channel: 1500ms — the Drake breathes fire, filling the cone with flame. Damage ticks every 375ms.

Recovery: 800ms — standard punish window

Cooldown: 7000ms

Direction lock: The cone aims where the target WAS at telegraph start. The Drake does NOT rotate during channel.

The player experience: An orange wedge flashes on the ground pointed at you — you have 0.6 seconds to sidestep. Then fire erupts and fills the wedge for 1.5 seconds. If you're caught in it, you take rapid ticks of damage plus a burn that keeps hurting after you escape. The Drake is locked in place during the channel, so flanking it is the play — get behind it and attack while it's breathing fire at empty space. The cone is wide enough (60°) that you must move decisively, not just shuffle sideways.

What makes cones different from every other shape: Circles (ground_slam) and lines (charge) are symmetrical — they look the same from any angle. Cones are directional and asymmetrical. The rendering math is more complex: you need to draw a pie-slice with an arc, the telegraph must aim in a specific direction, and the "am I inside the cone?" check requires an angle comparison, not just a distance check. Cones also introduce channeling — the ability deals damage continuously over time rather than in a single burst.

1 The Animation Lifecycle

Fire breath has a channel phase between telegraph and recovery. During the channel, the Drake is locked in place breathing fire. Damage ticks repeatedly — this is NOT a single burst like ground_slam.

TELEGRAPH Warning phase (600ms)

  • An orange cone outline appears on the ground — a pie-slice shape
  • The cone is 60° wide, 5 tiles long, centered on the Drake's facing direction toward the target
  • Direction locks immediately — the cone does NOT follow the player
  • The outline pulses and the fill opacity ramps from 0% to 25% over 600ms
  • The Drake plays a head-rear-back animation — inhaling, chest glowing
  • A rising hiss/roar builds — fire crackling mixed with reptilian growl

The 60° arc is the key readability challenge. The player must instantly understand the shape and whether they're inside it. The pulsing border and semi-transparent fill are essential — a thin outline alone isn't readable at game speed.

CHANNEL Active damage zone (1500ms)

  • The cone fills with animated fire — the telegraph shape becomes a damage zone
  • Fire streams outward from the Drake's mouth toward the cone edge
  • The fire visual billows and turbulates — not static, constantly shifting
  • Ember particles rise up from the fire zone
  • Damage ticks every 375ms (4 ticks total) — players inside take 8 damage per tick
  • First tick applies burn DoT (3 dmg/sec for 3 seconds)
  • The Drake is completely immobile — locked in place, can't turn, can't cancel
  • A constant roaring fire sound plays for the full 1.5 seconds

Players who dodge the telegraph see a clear "go behind it" opportunity. Players who get caught take escalating damage — the burn DoT means even escaping mid-channel still costs you. The longer you stay, the worse it gets.

RECOVERY The punish window (800ms)

  • Fire dissipates — the cone fades over 200ms with lingering embers
  • The Drake plays an exhausted/panting animation — head lowered, chest heaving
  • Takes 1.25x damage during this window
  • Cannot move or use abilities
  • Faint heat shimmer lingers in the cone area for 500ms after fire stops

The 1.5s channel + 0.8s recovery = 2.3 seconds of total vulnerability from the flank. This is the Drake's weakness — it commits fully to one direction.

Burn DoT (separate from the ability phases)

  • Applied on the first damage tick if the player is in the cone
  • Deals 3 damage per second for 3 seconds (9 total)
  • Visually: small flame particles orbit the burning player
  • The burn DoT is a status effect on the player — it persists independently of the ability
  • It does NOT stack if the player gets hit by multiple ticks — it refreshes duration instead
  • The burn is managed by the StatusEffectSystem, not the EffectAnimator or the cone

4 Visual Frame Breakdowns

A. Telegraph Frames (cone outline on ground — 600ms)

A 60° pie-slice that fades in, pulses, and grows slightly more opaque as it nears resolve. Direction is locked to the target's position at telegraph start.

B. Channel Frames (active fire filling the cone — 1500ms)

Fire streams outward from the Drake's mouth. Multiple particle layers create depth: a base glow, mid-layer flame tongues, and top-layer bright sparks. The fire billows with pseudo-random turbulence.

C. Dissipation Frames (fire fades — 200ms after channel ends)

The channel phase is new and complex. Unlike ground_slam (instant), charge (movement), or homing_orb (projectile), fire_breath has a sustained phase where the ability is actively dealing damage over time while rendering an animated fire effect. The cone shape stays identical — only the visual filling it changes. The data separates the base glow (static fill), flame streams (directional particles), and embers (floating sparks) into three layers that combine into the full fire visual.

Part B — The Controller: Managing channel state

// ─── In effect-animator.js ───
// New method for cone/channeled abilities

startConeEffect(abilityId, enemy, targetX, targetY, telegraphMs) {
  const id = this.nextId++;
  const config = ABILITY_EFFECTS[abilityId];
  if (!config) return -1;

  // Lock direction at this moment
  const angle = Math.atan2(targetY - enemy.worldY, targetX - enemy.worldX);
  const halfArc = (config.telegraph.arcDegrees / 2) * (Math.PI / 180);
  const radiusPx = config.telegraph.radius * TILE_SIZE;

  this.activeEffects.set(id, {
    id,
    abilityId,
    config,
    enemy,

    // ── Cone geometry (locked at creation) ──
    x: enemy.worldX,
    y: enemy.worldY,
    angle,             // Center direction of the cone
    halfArc,           // Half the arc width in radians
    radiusPx,          // Pixel radius

    // ── Phase tracking ──
    phase: 'telegraph',  // 'telegraph' | 'channel' | 'dissipate' | 'done'
    phaseTimer: 0,
    telegraphDuration: telegraphMs,

    // ── Channel state ──
    tickTimer: 0,       // Accumulator for damage ticks
    ticksDealt: 0,

    // ── Flame stream particles (created at channel start) ──
    flameStreams: [],
    embers: [],
    emberAccum: 0,
  });

  return id;
},

// ── In the update() method, add 'channel' and 'dissipate' cases: ──

case 'channel': {
  const chanCfg = effect.config.channel;

  // ── Fire ramp-up (first 300ms fire builds to full intensity) ──
  const intensity = Math.min(effect.phaseTimer / chanCfg.rampUpTime, 1.0);

  // ── Damage tick check ──
  effect.tickTimer += deltaTime;
  if (effect.tickTimer >= chanCfg.tickInterval && effect.ticksDealt < chanCfg.tickCount) {
    effect.tickTimer -= chanCfg.tickInterval;
    effect.ticksDealt++;

    // Check every player: are they inside the cone right now?
    for (const player of players) {
      if (this.isInsideCone(player.worldX, player.worldY, effect)) {
        // Apply tick damage
        EnemyAbilitySystem.applyDamage(player, effect.config.damage || 8, 'fire');

        // Apply burn on first hit (refresh on subsequent)
        if (effect.config.burn) {
          StatusEffectSystem.apply(player, {
            type: 'burn',
            damagePerSecond: effect.config.burn.damagePerSecond,
            duration: effect.config.burn.duration,
            refresh: effect.config.burn.refreshOnReapply,
          });
        }
      }
    }
  }

  // ── Update flame stream particles ──
  this.updateFlameStreams(effect, deltaTime, intensity);

  // ── Spawn embers ──
  this.updateEmbers(effect, deltaTime, intensity);

  // ── Channel complete? ──
  if (effect.phaseTimer >= chanCfg.duration) {
    effect.phase = 'dissipate';
    effect.phaseTimer = 0;
    AudioManager.play(effect.config.audio.channelEnd);
  }
  break;
}

case 'dissipate': {
  // Fade remaining particles, apply heat shimmer
  this.updateEmbers(effect, deltaTime, 0);  // intensity=0 → no new spawns

  if (effect.phaseTimer >= effect.config.dissipation.duration +
      effect.config.dissipation.heatShimmer.duration) {
    effect.phase = 'done';
  }
  break;
}

// ── Cone hit detection ──

isInsideCone(px, py, effect) {
  // Step 1: Distance check (must be within radius)
  const dx = px - effect.x;
  const dy = py - effect.y;
  const dist = Math.hypot(dx, dy);
  if (dist > effect.radiusPx) return false;

  // Step 2: Angle check (must be within the arc)
  const pointAngle = Math.atan2(dy, dx);
  let angleDiff = pointAngle - effect.angle;

  // Normalize to [-PI, PI]
  while (angleDiff > Math.PI) angleDiff -= Math.PI * 2;
  while (angleDiff < -Math.PI) angleDiff += Math.PI * 2;

  return Math.abs(angleDiff) <= effect.halfArc;
},
The isInsideCone check is the critical math. This two-step test (distance + angle) determines whether a player takes damage each tick. Getting it wrong means players get hit outside the visual or dodge inside it — both destroy trust. The angle normalization (wrapping to [-π, π]) handles the case where the cone crosses the 0°/360° boundary. Always test with cones aimed in all four cardinal directions and both diagonals.

Part C — The Renderer: Drawing the cone

// ─── In effect-renderer.js ───

drawConeTelegraph(ctx, effect, camX, camY) {
  const cfg = effect.config.telegraph;
  const progress = Math.min(effect.phaseTimer / effect.telegraphDuration, 1.0);

  // Fade in
  const fadeIn = Math.min(effect.phaseTimer / cfg.fadeInTime, 1.0);
  const pulse = fadeIn >= 1.0
    ? 0.7 + 0.3 * Math.sin(effect.phaseTimer / 1000 * cfg.pulseSpeed * Math.PI * 2)
    : fadeIn;

  const sx = effect.x - camX;
  const sy = effect.y - camY;

  // ── Draw the pie slice ──
  // This is the key shape: a filled arc from (angle - halfArc) to (angle + halfArc)
  // connected back to the center point by straight lines.

  ctx.save();

  // Filled cone
  ctx.globalAlpha = cfg.fillOpacity * progress * fadeIn;  // Opacity ramps up with telegraph
  ctx.fillStyle = cfg.color;
  ctx.beginPath();
  ctx.moveTo(sx, sy);  // Start at center (enemy position)
  ctx.arc(sx, sy, effect.radiusPx,
    effect.angle - effect.halfArc,   // Start angle
    effect.angle + effect.halfArc     // End angle
  );
  ctx.closePath();  // Closes back to center, forming the pie slice
  ctx.fill();

  // Border
  ctx.globalAlpha = pulse;
  ctx.strokeStyle = cfg.borderColor;
  ctx.lineWidth = cfg.borderWidth;
  ctx.stroke();

  ctx.restore();
},

// ── CHANNEL FIRE: the active flame visual ──

drawChannelFire(ctx, effect, camX, camY) {
  const chanCfg = effect.config.channel;
  const intensity = Math.min(effect.phaseTimer / chanCfg.rampUpTime, 1.0);

  const sx = effect.x - camX;
  const sy = effect.y - camY;

  // ── Layer 1: Base glow (same cone shape, filled solid) ──
  ctx.save();
  ctx.globalAlpha = chanCfg.baseFill.opacity * intensity;
  ctx.fillStyle = chanCfg.baseFill.color;
  ctx.beginPath();
  ctx.moveTo(sx, sy);
  ctx.arc(sx, sy, effect.radiusPx, effect.angle - effect.halfArc, effect.angle + effect.halfArc);
  ctx.closePath();
  ctx.fill();
  ctx.restore();

  // ── Layer 2: Flame stream particles ──
  // Each stream is a "flame tongue" that travels outward from the mouth
  // within the cone bounds. When it reaches the edge, it respawns at origin.
  for (const flame of effect.flameStreams) {
    const lifeRatio = flame.dist / effect.radiusPx;  // 0 at mouth, 1 at edge

    // Flames are brightest near the mouth, darker at the edges
    const colorIdx = Math.min(
      Math.floor(lifeRatio * chanCfg.flameStreams.colors.length),
      chanCfg.flameStreams.colors.length - 1
    );
    const color = chanCfg.flameStreams.colors[colorIdx];
    const alpha = (1.0 - lifeRatio * 0.6) * intensity;
    const size = chanCfg.flameStreams.sizeRange[0] +
      lifeRatio * (chanCfg.flameStreams.sizeRange[1] - chanCfg.flameStreams.sizeRange[0]);

    ctx.save();
    ctx.globalAlpha = alpha;
    ctx.fillStyle = color;
    ctx.fillRect(flame.x - camX - size/2, flame.y - camY - size/2, size, size);
    ctx.restore();
  }

  // ── Layer 3: Rising embers ──
  for (const e of effect.embers) {
    if (e.life <= 0) continue;
    const alpha = (e.life / e.maxLife) * intensity;
    ctx.save();
    ctx.globalAlpha = alpha;
    ctx.fillStyle = '#FFCC00';
    ctx.fillRect(e.x - camX - 1, e.y - camY - 1, 3, 3);
    ctx.restore();
  }
},

// ── Flame stream management ──

updateFlameStreams(effect, dt, intensity) {
  const cfg = effect.config.channel.flameStreams;

  // Initialize streams on first call
  if (effect.flameStreams.length === 0) {
    for (let i = 0; i < cfg.count; i++) {
      effect.flameStreams.push(this.spawnFlameStream(effect, Math.random()));
    }
  }

  // Update each stream
  for (const flame of effect.flameStreams) {
    // Move outward along the cone direction (with turbulence)
    flame.dist += cfg.speed * (dt / 1000);
    flame.turbOffset += flame.turbSpeed * (dt / 1000);

    // Calculate position: base direction + random angle offset + turbulence
    const flameAngle = effect.angle + flame.angleOffset;
    const turbX = Math.sin(flame.turbOffset) * cfg.turbulence;
    const turbY = Math.cos(flame.turbOffset * 1.3) * cfg.turbulence;

    flame.x = effect.x + Math.cos(flameAngle) * flame.dist + turbX;
    flame.y = effect.y + Math.sin(flameAngle) * flame.dist + turbY;

    // Respawn at origin when reaching edge
    if (flame.dist >= effect.radiusPx) {
      Object.assign(flame, this.spawnFlameStream(effect, 0));
    }
  }
},

spawnFlameStream(effect, initialProgress) {
  const halfArc = effect.halfArc;
  return {
    dist: initialProgress * effect.radiusPx,
    angleOffset: (Math.random() - 0.5) * 2 * halfArc,  // Random within arc
    turbOffset: Math.random() * Math.PI * 2,
    turbSpeed: 2 + Math.random() * 3,
    x: effect.x,
    y: effect.y,
  };
},

Part D — The Glue: Connecting to the ability system

// ─── In enemy-ability-system.js ───
// Channeled abilities use the existing executeAbility flow
// but the resolve triggers a channel phase instead of instant damage.

executeAbility(enemy, target, ability, abilitySet) {
  const telegraphTime = /* ... */;
  const recoveryTime = ability.recoveryTime || 600;

  abilitySet.cooldowns[ability.id] = ability.cooldown;
  abilitySet.globalCooldown = 1000;
  enemy.abilityState = 'telegraph';
  enemy.abilityLockout = true;

  let effectId;
  if (ability.shape === 'cone') {
    effectId = EffectAnimator.startConeEffect(
      ability.id, enemy, target.worldX, target.worldY, telegraphTime
    );
  } else if (ability.movementType === 'charge') {
    /* ... charge path ... */
  } else {
    /* ... circle/default path ... */
  }
  enemy._currentEffectId = effectId;

  const enemyId = enemy.id;
  setTimeout(() => {
    if (!enemy || enemy.hp <= 0 || enemy.isStunned || enemy.isRooted) {
      EffectAnimator.cancel(effectId);
      this.cleanupAbilityState(enemy);
      return;
    }

    if (ability.channelDuration) {
      // ── CHANNEL: transition from telegraph → channel ──
      // The EffectAnimator handles the visual transition internally.
      // Damage ticks happen inside the EffectAnimator update loop
      // (it calls EnemyAbilitySystem.applyDamage via the players reference).
      enemy.abilityState = 'channeling';
      EffectAnimator.resolveToChannel(effectId, {
        onChannelComplete: () => {
          this.enterRecovery(enemy, recoveryTime, enemyId);
        },
        onInterrupt: () => {
          // If the Drake is stunned DURING the channel, fire stops early
          EffectAnimator.forceDisssipate(effectId);
          this.enterRecovery(enemy, recoveryTime * 0.5, enemyId);
        }
      });

      AudioManager.play(ABILITY_EFFECTS[ability.id].audio.channelStart);
      AudioManager.playLoop(ABILITY_EFFECTS[ability.id].audio.channelLoop,
        ability.channelDuration);
    } else {
      // Instant abilities (ground_slam path)
      EffectAnimator.resolve(effectId);
      this.resolveAbility(enemy, target, ability);
      this.enterRecovery(enemy, recoveryTime, enemyId);
    }
  }, telegraphTime);
},
Channeled abilities CAN be interrupted mid-channel. If a player stuns the Drake while it's breathing fire, the channel stops early. The onInterrupt callback triggers early dissipation and a shorter recovery. This gives players a meaningful choice: dodge the breath and punish during recovery (safe) vs stun the Drake mid-breath to stop it early (aggressive, uses a cooldown but saves teammates who got caught).

drawGround(ctx, camX, camY) {
  for (const [id, effect] of EffectAnimator.activeEffects) {
    if (effect.phase === 'telegraph') {
      if (effect.config.telegraph.type === 'circle_grow') this.drawCircleTelegraph(/*...*/);
      else if (effect.config.telegraph.type === 'line_fixed') this.drawLineTelegraph(/*...*/);
      else if (effect.config.telegraph.type === 'cone_fixed') this.drawConeTelegraph(/*...*/);
    }
  }
},

drawOverlay(ctx, camX, camY) {
  for (const [id, effect] of EffectAnimator.activeEffects) {
    // Channel fire renders ON TOP of entities (engulfing)
    if (effect.phase === 'channel') {
      this.drawChannelFire(ctx, effect, camX, camY);
    }
    // Dissipation (fading fire + shimmer)
    if (effect.phase === 'dissipate') {
      const fadeAlpha = 1.0 - (effect.phaseTimer / effect.config.dissipation.duration);
      if (fadeAlpha > 0) {
        ctx.save();
        ctx.globalAlpha = fadeAlpha;
        this.drawChannelFire(ctx, effect, camX, camY);
        ctx.restore();
      }
    }
    // Standard impacts (ground_slam rings, charge shockwave, etc.)
    if (effect.phase === 'impact') {
      this.drawImpact(ctx, effect, camX, camY);
    }
  }
},

7 Adapting This for Other Cone / Channeled Abilities

frost_breath (Frost Elemental)

Identical shape, different visuals and effect. Change the arc to 75° (wider — frost breath fans out more than fire). Change colors to blue/white (#88CCFF, #AADDFF, #FFFFFF). Instead of burn DoT, apply slow (40% movement reduction for 2 seconds). The flame streams become ice crystal particles that drift more slowly. The dissipation leaves behind a frost ground texture instead of heat shimmer. Channel duration stays 1500ms. Mechanically identical, visually opposite.

poison_cloud (Plague Bearer)

90° arc, 4 tile radius — widest and shortest cone. Channel is only 800ms but the cloud lingers for 3 seconds after the channel ends as a persistent hazard zone. The cone transitions from "active spray" to "static cloud" — a semi-transparent green fog that doesn't animate directionally but slowly billows. The cloud deals 2 damage/sec to anyone standing in it. The linger mechanic means you need a hazardZone phase after dissipation that keeps the damage-check active even after the visual changes.

void_beam (Shadow Sentinel — if implemented as a cone variant)

A very narrow cone (15°) that functions more like a beam. Same rendering math — just a thin pie slice. The narrow arc makes it easier to dodge sideways but devastating if you're in a corridor. Channel is shorter (1000ms) with higher tick damage. The beam visual is a solid purple line with edge glow, not a particle field — use a beam sub-renderer that draws a filled gradient along the cone's center axis.
